01

Storefront & Shopping

The customer-facing side of the site — browsing, discovery, and checkout.

Server-rendered catalogHomepage and product pages render fresh on every visit, with pagination (5–100 per page), jump-to-page, and category & sort filters.
Search with autocompleteFuzzy suggestions as you type, so a slightly misspelled product name still finds its way home.
Product galleriesMulti-image flip/slide animation with hover and pinch zoom on the detail page.
Managed categoriesDiyas, Idols, Corporate Gifts, Pocket Temples, Gifts, Pan Stands — plus whatever gets added later — power both the storefront filter and the product form.
Cart & coupon codesSlide-out cart drawer with live coupon validation before the customer ever reaches payment.
Customer reviewsStar ratings on every product, shown only after an admin approves them.
WishlistSaved on-device, no account needed — heart icon on cards and a dedicated wishlist page.
Recently viewedA quiet strip of what you looked at last, stored locally.
Share & copy linkNative share sheet or a one-tap copy link on every product.
Dark modeFollows the system on first visit, remembers the choice after that, no flash of the wrong theme.
Promo bannerPublic coupon codes surface on-site; private codes stay reserved for WhatsApp and social outreach.
Everyday polishA branded 404 page, an installable PWA manifest, a cookie consent banner, and keyboard-focus styling throughout.
02

WhatsApp Communication

Order updates travel where customers actually read them.

Order alertsPlaced orders notify both the business number and the customer automatically via Green API.
Status updatesChanging an order's status in admin sends the customer a WhatsApp update — no separate email channel to check.
"Chat & ask for a discount"A direct WhatsApp line on every product page, before the customer even adds to cart.
Printable invoiceThe success page generates a GST-itemised invoice with your GSTIN, downloadable or printable on the spot.
03

Admin Panel

Password-gated, everything the business runs from day to day.

ProductsCreate and edit listings, multi-image galleries, and live inventory counts.
CategoriesAdd or remove the categories every product form and storefront filter draws from.
OrdersFull order list with status changes that trigger the WhatsApp update above.
CouponsFlat or percentage discounts, usage caps, expiry dates, and a public/private visibility switch.
Review moderationNothing reaches the storefront until it's approved here.
Low-stock alertsA heads-up before an item quietly runs out.
04

Payments & Tax

Live payments, priced and taxed correctly by default.

Razorpay checkoutLive keys, real transactions.rzp_live
GST invoicing5% GST is calculated back out of your GST-inclusive price and shown on the invoice — the customer never pays more than the sticker price.
Server-side pricingEvery price is recalculated from the database at payment time, regardless of what the browser sends.
05

Security & Data

The part customers never see, and the part that matters most.

Webhook signature verificationEvery payment webhook is checked with an HMAC signature before an order is created.HMAC-SHA256
Authentic order notesOrder details are read back from Razorpay's own stored record, never trusted from the request that arrives.
Row Level SecurityOrders, coupons, and categories are readable only by the server's service-role key — the public key the browser uses can't see them at all.RLS
Isolated service keyThe one key that bypasses those restrictions never ships to the browser, by construction.
Gated admin routesEvery admin API sits behind the same password check as the admin panel itself.
06

Quality Assurance

Small, but it catches real regressions.

Automated test suite15 tests covering payment-signature verification, coupon math, and GST calculation, run before anything ships.vitest
